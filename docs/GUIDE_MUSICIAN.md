# Musician's guide

You never need a password.

## Registering

Your director sends a link that looks like:

```
https://<ensemble>.scoreassign.com/register/registration
```

Fill it in once per season. The part that matters most is the instrument list:
**drag your instruments into the order you would rather play them**, best-loved
first. The assignment engine treats that order as a real preference, not a tie
breaker.

Also worth answering carefully:

- **Willing to play more than one instrument?** Say no and you will never be
  handed two different instruments — though you may still be asked to cover two
  parts on the same one.
- **Experience level** and **preferred difficulty** decide whether you are put on
  a first part with the exposed entrances or a supporting inner voice.
- **Maximum parts** is a hard ceiling. Nobody will assign you more.

## Seeing your parts

Go to `/musician/sign-in`, type the email address you registered with, and a
sign-in link arrives by email. It is valid for 30 minutes and works once. There
is nothing to remember and nothing to reset.

The link opens **My parts**: every song, instrument and part number you were
assigned, with the concert date.

## Confirming or declining

Each part has **Confirm** and **Decline**. Declining asks for a short note —
"away that weekend", "would rather play alto" — which your director sees on their
board. Confirming tells them the seat is settled.

You can change your mind by signing in again and answering differently, up until
the director closes the season.

## Your details

**My details** shows the record the ensemble holds for you: your name, email,
phone, instrument order and answers. Only you and your ensemble's staff can see
it. Other musicians never see your email address or phone number anywhere in the
app.

If something is wrong, tell your director — they can correct it for you.

## Trouble

- **No email arrived.** Check spam, then confirm with your director that the
  address they hold matches the one you typed. The link only goes to an address
  that is already on the roster.
- **"That link has expired."** Request another; they last 30 minutes.
- **The registration form says it is closed.** Registration has not opened yet
  for this season, or it has already closed. Ask your director.
